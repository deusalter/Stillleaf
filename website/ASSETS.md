# Asset provenance

- `public/covers/*.svg`: original procedural vector art made for this website. Titles are fictional decorative jackets; they do not depict books in the app or a bundled catalog. No external images, publisher jackets, or author endorsements.
- `public/leaf.svg`: approved Pageleaf identity, generated from `Sources/BooksPresence/Pageleaf.swift` by `scripts/generate-pageleaf.sh`. Shared with the native app/Dock and desktop reader. Header, footer, download mark and favicon use this asset. Historical screenshot assets below still show their recorded baseline; refresh them against the combined UI release, rather than retouching old screenshots.
- `public/favicon.ico`: the Pageleaf path in leaf green `#176650`, rendered at 32 × 32 by headless Chromium and wrapped as a PNG-in-ICO. `public/leaf.svg` stays the primary (adaptive) favicon.
- `public/apple-touch-icon.png` (180), `public/icon-192.png`, `public/icon-512.png`: resized with `sips` from `assets/BooksPresence.png`, the app icon generated by `scripts/generate-pageleaf.sh`. Regenerate them if the app icon changes. `public/site.webmanifest` lists the 192/512 icons.
- `public/og-image.jpg`: original 1200 × 630 social card made for this site: Pageleaf mark, wordmark and headline in the site's licensed fonts, with the original Quiet Hours, The Little Things and Tidelines jackets. It contains no app screenshot.
- `public/badges/app-store.svg` (not present): needed only when `site.ios.state` is `"available"`; the build fails without it. Use Apple's official “Download on the App Store” badge from Apple's marketing resources (App Store Marketing Guidelines), unmodified and at least 40 px tall. Don't draw or recolour a substitute.
- `public/daily-arc.svg`: original vector recreation of Stillleaf's native `DottedReadingArc` from `Sources/BooksPresence/ReadingProgress.swift`. Its 254×250 view follows the native two-row geometry: 37 outer dots, 31 inner dots, a 140° start and 260° sweep, and a partially filled leading dot. The static asset shows a synthetic 12-of-24-page day. `src/showcase.js` recreates these dots for the scroll-driven sample; neither version reads or records actual reading activity. The surrounding Today card is a website recreation with sample data, not a captured native screenshot.
- `public/screenshots/today.webp` and `library.webp`: genuine native app-owned renders with synthetic history from the baseline Stillleaf 1.5.0 / bundle 17, commit `0e23ba631891e47a6e44da93ea888e548d184bfa`. Source renders: original checkout `.local/design-journal-installed/today-light.png` and `library-light.png`, 2360×1640. Compressed to WebP at 1770×1230, preserving aspect ratio; no content altered.
- `public/screenshots/reviews.webp`: genuine `written-review-light.png` render from the same set, 1180×1080, compressed without resizing. This is the personal review editor, not a screenshot of a released built-in reader.

- Native render source: `Sources/BooksPresence/UIRender.swift` creates a temporary synthetic ReadingStore and sets `startTracking: false`. Screenshots contain sample data and generated placeholder covers, not a user's personal library or publisher artwork. Captions explicitly identify sample data.
- Instrument Serif and DM Sans are self-hosted from the pinned Fontsource packages. Their SIL Open Font License files are included at `public/licenses/`. Font licensing does not establish licensing of the Stillleaf application.
- The customizable reader in `index.html` is an illustrative website scene that follows the daily arc within one shared reading-journey stage. The wipe, perspective transforms, and transition to a forest background are website presentation effects. Its original passage, headed “A little room for the world,” and the fictional title “The quiet between things” were written for this demonstration. They are not an excerpt from a published book, a bundled EPUB, or evidence of shipped reader UI. The Quiet Hours cover in the daily card is likewise a fictional decorative jacket. The genuine native screenshot gallery is available in an optional details section beneath the editorial journal scene.
- Stillleaf, Warm, Dusk, and Night in `src/showcase-model.js` are four of the Mac reader's page colours (`THEMES` in `Reader/desktop/reader/src/appearance.js`, ids `paper`, `sepia`, `dusk`, `night`), with original descriptive text. `tests/pages.test.mjs` checks the colours still match. The reader has more themes; the site shows four. `src/showcase.css` defines paired ink and paper colors that switch together. Instrument Serif/DM Sans are the licensed fonts noted above; Georgia/Times New Roman are system-font fallbacks, not redistributed font files.
- The botanical leaves, orbit lines, page surfaces, and dimensional book faces are original CSS shapes. The leaves and orbit form one shared background behind the daily arc and reader. The mobile-roadmap phone symbol is an original inline vector. These decorative assets and the “Coming soon” mobile label do not establish a released Android or iOS build.

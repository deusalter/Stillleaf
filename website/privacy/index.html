Privacy policy

Your reading stays on your Mac.

Stillleaf is a reading journal, so it knows a lot about what you read. That information belongs on your Mac and nowhere else. This policy explains what the app stores, and the few times it talks to the internet.

Effective {{effective-date}}

In short

  • There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.
  • Your reading history, reviews, notes, and imported books are stored on your Mac.
  • {{publisher}} does not run servers that receive your reading data, and never sees it.
  • Stillleaf connects to the internet only for features you choose to use, listed below.

What Stillleaf stores on your Mac

Everything lives in one folder, ~/Library/Application Support/BooksPresence/. The folder keeps the app’s original name so upgrades keep your history.

  • Reading journal (history.sqlite): the books in your Library (title, author, and identifiers), reading sessions with their start and end times and time zone, page progress, goals, finished dates, ratings, written reviews, and any corrections you make.
  • Imported books (Publications/ and Audiobooks/): copies of the EPUB and audio files you import.
  • Reader state (ReaderState/): where you are in each book, your reader settings, notes, and bookmarks, plus saved page counts for each screen layout (ReaderState/PageMaps/), so a book you reopen shows its page count at once.
  • Covers (Covers/): cover images taken from your books, from Apple Books on your Mac, or from a cover search you started.
  • Settings: your preferences, kept in the standard macOS preferences for the app, including a Discord application ID if you enter one.

Stillleaf also keeps a health log of tracking problems, such as a permission that was turned off, so it can explain gaps in your history. It stays in the same folder.

Apple Books on your Mac

Stillleaf works with Apple Books in two separate ways. Neither sends anything over the internet.

  • Finished-book sync is on by default. Stillleaf reads Apple Books’ library database on your Mac, read-only, for titles, authors, covers, saved progress, and finished dates. On macOS 14 and later, macOS may ask whether Stillleaf may access data from other apps. You can turn this off in Settings, under Data & privacy, then Apple Books.
  • Reading-time tracking is optional and stays off until you give Stillleaf Accessibility permission in System Settings. When it is on, Stillleaf looks at the Apple Books window you are reading in: its title, the structure of the window, and the page numbers in its footer, to work out which book is open and when you turn a page. You can remove the permission at any time.

Stillleaf never reads the text of your books, takes screenshots, uses OCR, or looks at other apps’ windows, and never changes anything in Apple Books.

When Stillleaf uses the internet

Reading, goals, and your journal work entirely offline. Stillleaf makes a network connection only in these cases:

Every connection the Mac app can make
Feature What is sent To
Book search, when you add reading by hand and type a title The words you type, up to 120 characters. Cover images for the results are then downloaded. No cookies, account, or library contents are sent. Open Library (openlibrary.org and covers.openlibrary.org), run by the Internet Archive, which may serve covers from its own servers
Automatic public covers (off unless you turn on both Discord sharing and automatic cover art) A book’s title and author, to find one exactly matching public cover image for Discord. Your local covers are never uploaded. Apple’s public search service (itunes.apple.com)
Discord presence (off unless you turn it on) The title and author of the book you are reading, your page progress, and a public cover link if one is available. Sent to the Discord app on your Mac, which shows it on your profile. The Discord desktop app, then Discord’s servers
Update checks, when you choose Check for Updates, or once a day if you turn on Check automatically (off by default) One plain request for the release file at {{feed-path}} on this website, naming only the app version. It contains no reading data, identifiers, or cookies. Stillleaf never downloads or installs anything itself. This website

Like any internet request, these reveal your IP address to the service you contact. Open Library, Apple, and Discord handle that information under their own privacy policies. The built-in reader blocks books from loading anything from the internet, and the app contains no analytics, crash reporting, or advertising code.

Exporting and deleting your data

In Settings, under Data & privacy, you can export your journal as JSON or CSV, make and restore backups, delete individual books or sessions, or delete everything. Deleting everything removes your history, cached covers, and Stillleaf’s own backups from your Mac, moves imported books (with their notes and highlights) and audiobooks to the Trash, and turns off Apple Books sync. Empty the Trash to finish. Copies you saved somewhere else, such as exports, need to be removed separately. Removing a book from your Library never deletes the original file in Apple Books.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics, or third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from this site. If you pause the page’s animation, that choice is saved in your browser’s local storage and never sent anywhere. The company that hosts the site may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and browser types, to run and protect it.

Children

Stillleaf is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If a new version of Stillleaf changes what it stores or sends, this policy will be updated first, with a new effective date at the top of the page.

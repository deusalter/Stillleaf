Your reading
stays on your Mac.

No account, no analytics, no ads. Your history, reviews, and books are files on your Mac that you can export, back up, or delete.

Read the privacy policy

  • Nothing to sign up for

    Stillleaf has no accounts and no servers of its own. It works offline.

  • Apple Books, if you want it

    With Accessibility permission, Stillleaf can notice when you read in Apple Books. It never changes your Books library.

  • Discord, off by default

    Turn it on to show what you’re reading on your Discord profile. Turn it off and the card disappears.

  • Yours to take with you

    Export to JSON or CSV, make backups, or delete everything from Settings.

A few things
you might wonder.

Which books can I read in Stillleaf?+

DRM-free EPUB 2 and EPUB 3 files. Import them from Library and read offline. Books protected by DRM, including Apple Books Store purchases, can’t be opened. You can still track those in Apple Books.

Can I keep using Apple Books?+

Yes. Optional Apple Books tracking notices supported Apple Books reading windows once you allow Accessibility access. It pauses when it can’t identify the book reliably. You can also add reading sessions by hand, including paper books.

Does Stillleaf need the internet?+

No. Reading, goals, and your journal work offline. Searching for a book to add by hand asks Open Library, and the optional cover lookup asks Apple’s public catalogue. Both happen only when you use them.

How are pages and reading time counted?+

In the built-in reader, Stillleaf counts the pages you move through and the time you spend with the book open. It is a record of reading activity, not a measure of attention. Pick pages or minutes for your daily goal.

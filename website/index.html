you might wonder.

What can Stillleaf do today?+

The current native Mac app is a reading tracker and journal. It includes a Library, a completion Timeline, reading history, daily and yearly goals, personal written reviews, optional ratings, and optional Discord presence. The integrated EPUB reader is still in development.

Can I keep using Apple Books?+

Yes. The current Mac journal can track supported Apple Books reading windows with Accessibility permission. Tracking pauses when the reader cannot be identified reliably. Manual reading records are also available. These options remain alongside the built-in reader as it develops.

Where does my journal live?+

The current Mac journal stores reading history and written reviews locally on your Mac, with export and backup tools. Discord sharing is optional and off by default. These describe the current Mac app; details for future releases will be confirmed before download.

Can I read EPUBs in Stillleaf yet?+

Not yet. The built-in offline EPUB reader is in development for Mac and Windows. It is planned to import local EPUBs and use their embedded covers. There is no release date to announce yet.

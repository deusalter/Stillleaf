About and press

A little space for a life with books.

Stillleaf began as a way to keep a quiet record of reading on the Mac. It grew into a journal and a reader in one, made to stay out of the way.

About Stillleaf

Stillleaf lives in the Mac menu bar. Open a DRM-free EPUB in its built-in reader, or keep reading in Apple Books, and it keeps a gentle record: pages and minutes toward a daily goal, books toward a yearly one, the date you finished each book, and the thoughts you want to keep. Everything stays on your Mac. There is no account to create and nothing to sync.

Descriptions

One line

A quiet reading journal and EPUB reader for your Mac.

Short

Stillleaf is a reading journal for the Mac menu bar with a built-in offline EPUB reader. Set a daily goal in pages or minutes, keep a Timeline of finished books, and write private reviews. Your reading stays on your Mac.

Long

Stillleaf makes room for reading. It sits in the Mac menu bar and keeps a calm record of the books you spend time with. Read DRM-free EPUBs in its built-in reader, with page colours from soft green to true black, or let it notice when you read in Apple Books. Goals in pages or minutes fill a dotted ring through the day. Library gathers your books, Timeline remembers the ones you finish, History shows every day, week, month and year, and Reviews holds the words you want to keep. There are no accounts, analytics or ads: your journal is a set of files on your Mac that you can export or delete at any time.

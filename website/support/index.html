Support

How can we help?

Answers for Stillleaf for Mac. If you don’t find what you need, write to {{contact}} and we’ll get back to you.

Getting started

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or later. Apple Books tracking and Discord presence are optional and have their own requirements, described below.

Where is Stillleaf after I open it?

In the menu bar. Click the leaf to see today’s reading and open your journal. A Dock icon and the usual Mac menus appear while the journal or a book is open. Move the app to your Applications folder before turning on Open at login or Accessibility permission.

Can I see the welcome tour again?

Yes. Open Settings, choose Data & privacy, and select Welcome tour under Help.

Reading in Stillleaf

How do I add books?

Choose Import EPUBs in Library, or at the end of the welcome tour. Stillleaf copies the files into its own library, so you can open them offline. Your originals are left where they were.

Why won’t a book open?

Stillleaf reads DRM-free EPUB 2 and EPUB 3 books. Books protected by DRM, including purchases from the Apple Books Store, can’t be opened. Very large or damaged files are also refused, with a message that says why. You can still track DRM-protected books in Apple Books, or add the time by hand.

Can I change how books look?

Yes. In the reader, choose a page colour, typeface, text size, margins and line spacing, or switch between pages and continuous scrolling. Night is true black for dark rooms. Original keeps the publisher’s styling.

How are pages and time counted?

Stillleaf counts the pages you move through and the time you spend with a book open. It records reading activity; it can’t tell whether you were paying attention. Choose pages or minutes for your daily goal in Settings. You can add an optional yearly books goal too.

Can I record paper books or audiobooks?

Yes. Use Add reading for a paper book or a session you forgot, or Start manual reading to time one now. Local audiobooks can be imported in Library and played in Stillleaf.

Apple Books

How do I turn on Apple Books tracking?

  1. In Stillleaf’s reading settings, open Optional Apple Books integration and choose to allow access.
  2. In System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, then Accessibility, and turn on Stillleaf.
  3. Open a book in Apple Books. If nothing appears, quit and reopen Stillleaf.

Tracking stopped after an update

macOS sometimes forgets the permission when an app is replaced. In Accessibility settings, turn Stillleaf off and on again, then reopen it.

Why does tracking pause?

Stillleaf only counts reading when it can be sure which book is open. It pauses for windows it doesn’t support, when the Mac is locked or asleep, and when the permission is missing. The troubleshooting panel in Settings explains the current reason, and Health lists any gaps.

Discord

How do I show my reading on Discord?

Discord presence is off by default. Create an application in the Discord Developer Portal, copy its Application ID into Stillleaf’s Discord settings, and turn sharing on. The Discord desktop app needs to be running on the same Mac. The card shows the book’s title, author and page progress, and disappears when you stop reading, lock your Mac, or turn sharing off.

Your data

Where is my journal stored?

On your Mac, in ~/Library/Application Support/BooksPresence/. See the privacy policy for everything it contains.

How do I back up or move to a new Mac?

In Settings, under Data & privacy, create a backup or export your journal as JSON. Restore it on the other Mac from the same place. Cover images are separate files in the Covers folder; copy it too if you want your artwork.

How do I delete my data or uninstall?

Delete all data, in Settings under Data & privacy, removes your history from this Mac. Uninstall turns off Open at login, moves the app to the Trash and quits. Uninstalling keeps your history in case you come back, so delete your data first if you want it gone.

Contact us

Write to {{contact}}. Tell us which versions of Stillleaf and macOS you use. For tracking problems, a diagnostic report helps. Run this in Terminal and attach the output:

/Applications/Stillleaf.app/Contents/MacOS/books-diagnostic

The report describes permissions and the state of the Apple Books window. It leaves out book titles unless you add --include-metadata, so check it before sharing.
